# Privacy and source policy

The catalog requires no account and collects no application details. Applications happen on the original publisher's website.

## Attribution and reuse

Every record credits and links to its source. Titles preserve source language, while records avoid publisher descriptions and article prose. Publishers remain authoritative for deadlines and application conditions. Collection needs a source-specific access and reuse assessment; public availability is not automatic permission to reproduce content.

## Analytics

Optional Google Analytics 4 is configured for this site. The Google tag is loaded only after the visitor selects Allow analytics; Decline leaves it unloaded. Analytics measures page visits, category/country catalog views and navigation, country-filter selections, and browser/device information to help improve the index. Our custom events send only validated category/country identifiers and page numbers, never search terms or free-text input. The configured page URL omits query strings and fragments. We do not send application details, names or email addresses in our tracking code. Google processes analytics data under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Your choice is stored in this browser under `youthopp-analytics`. Clear this site's browser storage to reset the choice; declining afterward prevents future tag loading. The GA4 account owner controls retention and access in Google Analytics; the repository does not establish or claim a specific account retention period. For privacy questions, contact maintainers through the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/YouthOpp/youthopp.github.io/issues), without publishing personal information.

## Public contributions

GitHub logins and public contribution counts may appear on the contributors page. These are activity indicators, not skill assessments. Request corrections through the project issue tracker.

## Source removal

Publishers and other affected parties can request removal of a source or indexed link. The [source removal process](/docs/source-removal/) explains what to provide and how maintainers review an issue or pull request. Do not put personal, confidential or sensitive information in a public request.

## External websites

External sources have their own privacy and usage policies.
