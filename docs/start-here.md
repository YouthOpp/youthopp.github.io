# Start here

Good opportunities should be easier to find. YouthOpp brings scattered listings together, then sends you to the original publisher.

## Our mission

Help students, young people and recent graduates discover scholarships, internships, learning opportunities and early career programmes with less searching.

## Our vision

A useful public resource built openly by people learning and working together. Contributors can practise responsible AI development through real work. Participation does not guarantee a scholarship or a job.

## Our approach

- **Source first.** Credit the publisher and link to the original page.
- **Honest information.** Missing facts stay unknown. A recent collection does not prove applications are open.
- **Simple by design.** Make useful information easy to find and understand. No account needed.
- **Human accountability.** AI helps with development; people review changes and decide what ships.

Public benefit is our purpose, not a claim of registered charitable status. Listing a publisher does not imply partnership or endorsement.
